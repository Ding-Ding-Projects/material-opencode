Material Expressive design

The look

The app opens in the Material Expressive design: tonal surfaces on the default theme, Roboto Flex for interface text, large rounded panels, pill buttons and tabs, and spring motion on presses and switches. Home and Settings have slowly drifting backdrop graphics.

Material styling covers the buttons, switches, text fields, segmented buttons, checkboxes, menus, tooltips and the chat composer and bubbles. Some surfaces, such as the session side panels and dialog content, still keep their original layout.

Switching design

Choose Material Expressive or Classic in Settings › Appearance › Design language. Classic gives you the original OpenCode look back.

Picking another theme keeps that theme's colours. Picking a UI font replaces Roboto Flex.

Motion and reduced motion

Entrance, press, hover and backdrop motion are used on Home, Settings and the Configuration studio; not every surface has motion yet. Turn on Reduce motion, or use your system's reduced-motion preference, and every animation stops.
